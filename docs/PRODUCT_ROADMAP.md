# Product roadmap

## Release 0.1 — prove workflow continuity

Status: implemented in this repository.

- Transparent configuration and estimate
- Lead capture and consent
- Staff pipeline and ownership
- Site-visit scheduling
- Notes and activity history

Success test: a real staff member can process ten synthetic inquiries without using a separate spreadsheet to remember the next action.

## Release 0.2 — validate operations

Build only after Sambandha staff use v0.1.

- Verified pricing catalog managed by authorized staff
- Required follow-up dates and overdue queue
- Email acknowledgement and internal notification
- Export for weekly sales review
- Loss reasons and funnel metrics

Evidence required: repeated use, missed follow-ups, or measurable time spent copying information.

## Release 0.3 — verified proposal workflow

- Site-measurement record
- Material and exclusion checklist
- Proposal versions and approvals
- Customer-visible proposal link
- PDF output and acceptance history

Do not present a preliminary online estimate as a final contract.

## Later possibilities

- Private customer project dashboard
- Milestones, variation orders, and approvals
- Photo and drawing uploads
- Purchasing and vendor coordination
- AI-assisted design concepts with explicit labeling and human review

## Explicitly deferred

- Marketplace features
- Public vendor onboarding
- Customer payments
- Automated “final” AI quotations
- Full construction accounting

These expand risk and operational scope without first proving that the intake and follow-up workflow is valuable.
